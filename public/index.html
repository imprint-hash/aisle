Run a live honest ranking

Pick a shopping task and a sponsor. Aisle asks SERV Reasoning to rank six real weather services from Kite's catalogue twice: once with the sponsor hidden, once shown. If the two choices differ, sponsorship influenced the agent, and Aisle refuses to bill or buy.

Impact · business model

Who wins, and how Aisle earns

People

Your attention pays you

Today your attention earns money for ad platforms, and you get nothing. With Aisle, every time a brand pays for your agent's honest look, you get 60%, in USDC, straight to your Kite wallet. You don't need a bank card or a reward programme, and you never have to buy anything.

Brands

Stop paying for bots

Brands only pay for a view from a Kite-registered agent working for a real owner. Fake agents get $0 (0 of 10 paid in our tests). A view that sponsorship influenced isn't billed either.

Agent shopping

Agents stay on your side

When agents shop for us, ads will try to buy their choices. Aisle lets brands pay to be considered, never chosen: 0 of 30 choices changed by sponsorship, and the sponsor still won on merit 5 of 5 times when it was really best.

How the money moves

Brand setsa price per verified view and a budget. Pays only for views that pass every rule.
60% shopperthe person whose agent did the looking
25% Aisleour revenue, taken from every verified view
15% developerwhoever built the shopping agent, so agent makers want to plug in

Example: a $500 campaign at $0.05 per view buys 10,000 verified views. $300 goes to shoppers, $125 to Aisle and $75 to agent developers. Nothing is paid for bots or bought choices.

    Paid on Kite's own chain · kite-x402-hono (Kite priority repo)

    Paid per view, on Kite's own chain

    Aisle's payout service runs Kite's official kite-x402-hono wrapper at /v1/consider. The brand's agent pays $0.05 in pieUSD on Kite testnet through x402. The wrapper settles through Kite's facilitator only if Aisle's check passes: a registered agent and an honest choice. A fake agent gets a 403, and the brand is not charged.

    How it works

    Considered, not bought

    01

    Brand funds a campaign

    Price per verified view and a total budget. The ad text is untrusted: SERV screens it, and manipulative copy is refused before anything is billed.

    02

    Only real agents earn

    The shopper's agent must be a Kite-registered agent linked to an authorised, passkey-backed Kite account. Unregistered agents earn nothing.

    03

    Ranked on facts, twice

    SERV ranks structured facts (price, coverage, days, freshness) with Multipath and checks itself with Shadow Agent, once blind and once with the sponsor shown. Same answer, or no bill.

    04

    Real payments through Kite

    The agent buys the winner through its Kite Passport spending session. The brand settles verified views through a Kite wallet transfer approved by passkey: 60% to the shopper, 25% to Aisle, 15% to the agent's developer.
